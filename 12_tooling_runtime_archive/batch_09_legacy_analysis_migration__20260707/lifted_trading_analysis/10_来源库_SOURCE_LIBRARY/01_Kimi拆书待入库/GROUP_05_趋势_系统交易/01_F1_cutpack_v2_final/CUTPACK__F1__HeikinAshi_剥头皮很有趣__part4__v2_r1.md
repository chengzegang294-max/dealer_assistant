# CORE_CONCEPTS

1. **Trade Only When It Fits**: Successful traders decide when NOT to trade. Conditions must be favorable. Avoid boredom trading and overtrading. Trading is not a 9-to-5 job.
2. **Avoidance Calendar for Forex**:
   - Bank holidays (UK, US, and currency-specific holidays reduce liquidity)
   - Friday afternoons (weekend gap risk, institutions closing positions)
   - Market open/close minutes (illiquid, thin order books, wider spreads, unexpected volatility)
   - Monday morning first minutes (Friday closers reopening, unexpected gaps)
   - Winter and summer holidays (lower volume from big firms)
   - Asian session (unless specifically trading Asian equities; low liquidity for FX compared to London/NY)
   - Hours before major economic data releases (low volatility, ranging, then explosive post-release moves)
3. **Best Forex Session**: London-NY overlap (08:00-11:00 EST / 13:00-16:00 GMT) has the highest volatility. London alone handles ~40.9% of global FX volume; NY ~18.9%. London opening (08:00 GMT pre-market) also sees volatility increase.
4. **Best Index Session**: Pre-market (08:00-09:00) absorbs overnight news; first hour (09:00-10:00) often sets daily high/low. European indices best in morning; US indices in afternoon (for European traders). US markets are most independent.
5. **Best Oil Session**: "Golden hour" 08:50-10:30 EST (13:50-15:30 GMT). Avoid NY open first minute. Wednesday 10:30 EST: US crude oil inventories report — wait for release, then trade the following hour.
6. **Key Economic Data Hierarchy (Highest Impact)**: Monetary policy (central bank statements, press conferences), labor market (NFP — first Friday of month 08:30 EST, German unemployment), leading indicators (German Ifo, US ISM PMI), consumer confidence, GDP, CPI, PPI.
7. **Economic Calendar Source**: www.forexfactory.com. Yellow/Orange icons = low impact. Red icon = high impact. Red events = avoid trading around them. Specific example: 2015-10-14 had GBP Average Earnings Index, GBP Claimant Count, GBP Unemployment Rate at 10:30; CHF ZEW Expectations, EUR Industrial Production at 11:00; USD Core Retail Sales, USD PPI, USD Retail Sales at 14:30 (all GMT).
8. **Post-Release Trading**: Wait for the data release, then trade the directional wave. Do not trade the pre-release calm (ranging, low volatility). Market reaction is often immediate and can be 50-120+ pips in seconds/minutes. Example: EUR/USD on 2015-10-14 ranged <10 pips from 10:00-13:30 GMT waiting for US retail sales; then became active after 13:30.
9. **Dynamic Position Sizing (The Core Money Management Rule)**:
   - After 2 consecutive losses: halve position size (e.g., 5 lots → 2 lots). Two losses = warning that system is mismatched with current market.
   - After 7 consecutive wins: increase position size (e.g., 2 lots → 5 lots). Seven wins = market is highly favorable.
   - This is "offense when winning, defense when losing."
   - Never risk > 1% of capital per trade (for beginners). Some experienced traders have natural limits (e.g., 2 E-mini contracts max) that should be respected.
10. **Account as the Ultimate Control Tool**: The account balance/equity curve is the best feedback. Track equity curve hourly/daily. If after 20 trades profits turn into increasing losses, stop and take a break.
11. **Flow State Philosophy**: Trading success comes from a state of flow (clear goals, total focus, control, skill-challenge match, no fear). Eliminate analysis paralysis. Speed > perfection. Experience is in STOP management, not prediction.
12. **Warning Signals to Stop Trading**:
   - First loss after a long winning streak (e.g., after 28 wins) = tired or market weakening. Stop.
   - Sudden slowdown in market after a fast session = take a break.
   - Extreme volatility (e.g., 50 pips in 1 second during 2011 euro crisis) = reduce to 1/10th size or stop.
   - Boredom, distraction, frustration = stop.
13. **Technical Analysis Critique**: The author argues that TA is backward-looking, creates bias, and cannot predict the future. Patterns are self-defeating because everyone knows them. The only real skill is reactive stop management and trading what you see in the moment, not what you predict.
14. **Weekend Gap Risk**: EUR/USD and other FX pairs can gap significantly between Friday close and Sunday open due to weekend events (elections, crises, natural disasters, terrorist attacks).

# FORMULAS_AND_ALGOS

- **Consecutive Win/Loss Streak**:
  - Streak_wins = count of consecutive trades with PnL > 0 (resets to 0 on first loss)
  - Streak_losses = count of consecutive trades with PnL < 0 (resets to 0 on first win)
- **Dynamic Position Size**:
  - Base_size = calculated from 1% risk rule
  - If Streak_losses >= 2: Size = Base_size / 2
  - If Streak_losses >= 4: Size = Base_size / 4 (or stop trading)
  - If Streak_wins >= 7: Size = min(Base_size × 2.5, Max_comfortable_size)
  - Max_risk_per_trade = Account_balance × 0.01
- **Session Time Filter (FX, EUR/USD)**:
  - Trade_window = (Time >= 08:00 EST) AND (Time <= 11:00 EST) AND (Not Friday_after_12:00 EST)
  - No_trade = Monday_first_30_min OR Pre_holiday OR Pre_NFP_window
- **Pre-Release ATR Calm Detector**:
  - Calm = (ATR_1m < 0.3 × ATR_20m) AND (within 60 minutes of scheduled red event)
  - Block_entries = Calm
- **Post-Release Trade Window**:
  - After_release = (Time >= Event_time + 2 minutes) AND (Time <= Event_time + 30 minutes)
  - Trade_allowed = After_release AND HA_trend_clear
- **Equity Curve Stop**:
  - Peak_equity = max(Account_balance over current session)
  - Drawdown_pct = (Peak_equity - Current_equity) / Peak_equity
  - Stop_trading = (Drawdown_pct > 0.02) AND (Trade_count_today >= 20)
- **First-Loss-After-Streak Stop**:
  - Warning = (Streak_wins_before_loss > 15) AND (Last_trade_PnL < 0)
  - If Warning: Stop_trading = True; review mental state
- **Volatility Spike Emergency**:
  - Spike = (Bar_range_1s > 50 pips) OR (Bar_range_1m > 5 × ATR_20m)
  - If Spike: Reduce_size_to = Base_size / 10; or Stop_trading = True
- **NFP Filter**:
  - No_trade_NFP = (Day_of_week == Friday) AND (Week_of_month == 1) AND (Time >= 06:30 EST) AND (Time <= 08:35 EST)
- **Oil Inventory Filter**:
  - No_trade_oil_Wed = (Day_of_week == Wednesday) AND (Time >= 09:30 EST) AND (Time <= 10:35 EST)
  - Resume_oil = (Time >= 10:35 EST) AND (Time <= 11:30 EST)

# NOT_QUANT_YET

1. **"Flow" State Detection**: The author's central philosophy is trading in a "flow" state. This is a psychological concept (Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi) that cannot be directly quantified. Proxies: consecutive wins, low decision time, no hesitation. But no direct measurement.
2. **Mental State Assessment**: The author advises adjusting position size based on mental state (e.g., bad mood = be defensive). No quantifiable proxy for mental state beyond self-assessment.
3. **Technical Analysis Bias Quantification**: The author argues that TA creates bias and is self-defeating. This is a philosophical critique, not a quantifiable rule. No proxy for "bias level" is provided.
