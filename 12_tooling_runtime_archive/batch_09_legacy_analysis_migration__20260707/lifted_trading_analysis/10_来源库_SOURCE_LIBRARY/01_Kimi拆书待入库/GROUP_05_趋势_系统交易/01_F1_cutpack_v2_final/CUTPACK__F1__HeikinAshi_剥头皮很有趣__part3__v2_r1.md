# CORE_CONCEPTS

1. **Trading as a Probability Game**: Scalping is a probability game. The trader must think in statistical terms: total of winners, total of losers, averages, ratios.
2. **The Four Key Metrics**:
   - **Win Rate** = Winning Trades / Total Trades
   - **Average Win** = Sum of Profits / Number of Winning Trades (in pips or currency)
   - **Average Loss** = Sum of Losses / Number of Losing Trades (in pips or currency)
   - **Reward Ratio** = Average Win / Average Loss
   - **Expected Value per Trade** = (Win Rate × Average Win) - (Loss Rate × Average Loss)
3. **The Win Rate Trap**: A high win rate (e.g., 62.50%) does NOT guarantee profitability if the average loss is much larger than the average win (reward ratio < 1). Jenny's week 1: 62.5% win rate, +14.8 pips gross, but -42.17 EUR net after commissions. The win rate alone is misleading.
4. **Reward Ratio > 1 is Essential**: The reward ratio must be stable above 1.0, combined with a win rate above 50%, to have a sustainable profitable system. Jenny's RR evolution: 0.61 (W1) → 0.76 (W2) → 1.50 (W3) → 0.79 (W4) → 0.75 (W5) → 0.66 (W6) → 1.05 (W7) → 1.20 (W8) → 0.38 (W9) → 1.16 (W10) → 1.53 (W11) → 1.85 (W12).
5. **Expected Value Must Exceed Costs**: Even with RR > 1, the expected value per trade must exceed the cost per trade (spread + commission). Jenny's W1 EV was 0.15 pips — below the spread (0.2-0.4 pips) + commission. W12 EV 1.78 pips finally exceeded total cost.
6. **Commissions are the Hidden Killer**: At 2.42 EUR per round-trip mini lot, commissions accumulate rapidly. Jenny's week 1: 32 trades → 113.02 EUR commissions, turning a 14.8 pip gross profit into a -42.17 EUR net loss. The author emphasizes negotiating with brokers for better rates as volume increases.
7. **Stop Loss Discipline**: Moving stop loss ("letting losses run") is the deadliest sin. Jenny's -25 pip loss in W1 came from moving her stop. If she had kept the 10-pip stop, her week 1 profit would have been >30 pips instead of 14.8.
8. **Time Stop**: A time-based stop ("if the expected scenario doesn't happen quickly, exit") was introduced in W12 and helped reduce average losses and improve RR to 1.85.
9. **Breakeven Stop Trap**: Moving stop to breakeven too early (when only a few pips profitable) is harmful. In W10, the author instructed Jenny to stop doing this, and her results immediately improved. The reason: small profits that could have grown were cut off prematurely.
10. **Learning Curve with Volume**: 1000 trades in ~3 months. High trade frequency accelerates learning. Jenny's 12-week progression shows the value of a trading journal and weekly statistical review.
11. **Position Size Fixed During Learning**: Jenny used 10,000 USD (1 mini lot) fixed size during the learning phase. The author explicitly avoided discussing position scaling in this book to keep the statistics clean. Scaling up should only happen after consistent profitability is achieved net of costs.
12. **Single Pair Focus**: Jenny switched from multiple pairs to focusing only on EUR/USD for better liquidity and narrower spreads. This simplified her learning curve.
13. **Final State (Post-12 Weeks)**: Jenny became a profitable scalper trading multiple standard lots. RR stabilized 1.50-1.90, win rate 55-60%, trading for a living. She negotiated better commission rates with her broker.

# FORMULAS_AND_ALGOS

- **Win Rate (WR)**:
  - WR = N_win / N_total
- **Average Win (AW)**:
  - AW = sum(PnL_i for all PnL_i > 0) / N_win
- **Average Loss (AL)**:
  - AL = sum(abs(PnL_i) for all PnL_i < 0) / N_loss
- **Reward Ratio (RR)**:
  - RR = AW / AL
  - Profitable_condition = (RR > 1.0) AND (WR > 0.50)
- **Expected Value per Trade (EV)**:
  - EV = (WR × AW) - ((1 - WR) × AL)
  - In pips: EV_pips = (WR × AW_pips) - ((1 - WR) × AL_pips)
- **Net P&L after Commission**:
  - Net_PnL = Gross_PnL - (Commission_per_trade × N_total)
  - In pips: subtract commission converted to pips (depends on pip value and lot size)
- **Commission in Pips (EUR/USD, 1 mini lot)**:
  - Pip_value_mini = 1 USD per pip ≈ 0.88 EUR (varies with EUR/USD rate)
  - Commission_pips ≈ 2.42 / 0.88 ≈ 2.75 pips per round-trip
  - Total_cost_pips = spread_pips + commission_pips ≈ 0.3 + 2.75 = 3.05 pips per trade
- **EV Viability Check**:
  - Viable = EV_pips > Total_cost_pips
  - Jenny W1: EV = 0.15 pips << 3.05 pips → NOT viable
  - Jenny W12: EV = 1.78 pips > 3.05 pips? → Borderline; higher lot size or better commission needed for comfortable margin
- **Slippage Flag**:
  - Slippage_pips = actual_loss_pips - planned_stop_pips
  - Flag = Slippage_pips > 1.0 (indicates stop moved or excessive slippage)
- **Breakeven Opportunity Cost**:
  - trades_closed_at_breakeven = count(0 <= PnL_pips <= 2)
  - subsequent_max_reachable = max(PnL_pips in next 5 bars after entry) for those trades
  - opportunity_cost = subsequent_max_reachable - 0

  - Week | Trades | WR% | AW | AL | RR | EV | Gross | Commission | Net
  - Compare own weekly row to Jenny's W1-W12 table to gauge learning curve position

# NOT_QUANT_YET

1. **Psychological Factors (Fear of Loss, Need to Win)**: Jenny's behavior patterns (taking small profits to "avoid losing", moving stops to avoid being wrong) are behavioral and require psychological coaching, not just metrics.
2. **Discipline Measurement**: The author observes discipline subjectively in weekly meetings. No automated proxy for "discipline" exists beyond stop-loss adherence.
3. **Negotiating Commission Rates**: The business advice to negotiate with brokers is real-world operational, not a quantifiable trading rule.
4. **Time Stop Exact Rule**: The author says "if expected scenario doesn't happen quickly, exit" but does not specify exactly how many minutes or what price condition constitutes "not happening." This is discretionary.
5. **"Feeling" the Market State**: Jenny's coach makes subjective judgments about whether a day is suitable for trading. No quantifiable proxy.
6. **Image Note**: The book contains 40 pages with many chart images (Jenny's weekly trade tables and statistics charts). EPUB extraction recovered the statistical summary tables but the visual bar-by-bar trade logs remain corrupted by OCR noise. The exact bar-by-bar P&L sequence for each week is partially lost due to column misalignment and symbol corruption in the EPUB source.
