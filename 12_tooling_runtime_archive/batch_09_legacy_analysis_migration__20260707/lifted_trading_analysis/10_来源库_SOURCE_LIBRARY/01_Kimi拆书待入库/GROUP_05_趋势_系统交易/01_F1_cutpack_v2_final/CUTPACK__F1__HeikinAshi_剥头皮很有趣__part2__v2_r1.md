# CORE_CONCEPTS

1. **Heikin Ashi Trend Progression**: A trend typically starts with small HA candles (hesitation), then large candles (fully developed trend), then small candles again + doji (exhaustion/turn). Color change confirms new cycle.
2. **Doji and Spinning Top as Entry Signals**: After a strong move, a doji or spinning top indicates momentum pause → potential counter-trend entry. Doji = balance between buyers/sellers, likely trend change. Spinning top = first sign of weakening trend.
3. **Counter-Trend Scalping Philosophy**: The HA scalper is a "trend-end expert." Identifies when momentum is strong (large candles) vs. weak (small candles, long wicks, doji). Never enter counter-trend when trend is at full strength (party at its peak).
4. **Color Change = Exit**: Core rule remains from Part 1 — hold until opposite color appears. No premature exit needed.
5. **Price Targets via Technical Analysis**: When combined with TA, targets can be set at: (a) support/resistance levels, (b) previous day's swing highs/lows, (c) round numbers (integer levels like 1.1200, 187.00, 119.00). Target orders automatically lock in profits.
6. **Dynamic Timeframe Selection**: 1-minute is default. Switch to 30-second when volatility is high; 2-minute when volatility is low. The experienced scalper manually adjusts chart timeframe during the session based on market conditions.
7. **Time-Based Stop Tightening**: If the expected scenario doesn't play out within minutes, move stop closer to entry (time stop). The probability of success decreases minute by minute if the market doesn't move immediately in the expected direction.
8. **Trend Day Identification**: Clues: (a) 2-3 prior range days increase trend-day probability; (b) market first moves in the opposite direction early in the session (false move); (c) major economic data or central bank events scheduled. Trend days often see 100-150 pip moves in EUR/USD.
9. **Risk/Reward Ratio**: Typically 1:1 for scalping. Example from live session: 7-pip stop, 13-pip target = ~1:1.8 (good). 10-pip stop, 11-pip target = ~1:1 (acceptable but difficult). Tighten stop to breakeven once some profit is captured to make it a "free trade."
10. **Higher Timeframe Context**: Always check daily/4H trend before scalping. If daily trend is up, prefer long scalps; if down, prefer short scalps. Don't fight the dominant trend all day.

# FORMULAS_AND_ALGOS

- **Doji on HA**:
  - Doji = (HA_body < 0.1 × ATR_14) AND (max(HA_upper_wick, HA_lower_wick) < 0.15 × ATR_14)
- **Spinning Top on HA**:
  - Spinning_Top = (HA_body < 0.15 × ATR_14) AND (HA_upper_wick > 2 × HA_body) AND (HA_lower_wick > 2 × HA_body)
- **Strong Trend Detection**:
  - Consecutive_same_color_count >= 3
  - Average_body_last_3 > 1.5 × SMA(HA_body, 20)
- **Momentum-End Entry (Counter-Trend)**:
  - Condition = Strong_Trend AND (Doji OR Spinning_Top) AND next_bar_color_flip
- **Round Number Proximity**:
  - Round_number_grid = 0.01 for most FX pairs (e.g., 1.1200, 1.1300); 1.00 for JPY pairs (e.g., 119.00, 120.00)
  - Proximity = min(abs(price - round_number)) across round numbers near current price
  - Near_round = Proximity < 5 pips (or 0.0005 / 0.05)
- **RRR Calculation**:
  - RRR = abs(Target - Entry) / abs(Entry - Stop)
  - Target = nearest_round_number OR nearest_support_resistance OR previous_swing
- **Time-Decay Stop**:
  - If minutes_since_entry > 3 AND unrealized_pips < 2:
    - Move stop to Entry ± 2 pips (breakeven zone)
- **Trend Day Filter (Simple)**:
  - Prior_2_days_range = (Day1_High - Day1_Low < 1.2 × ATR_20) AND (Day2_High - Day2_Low < 1.2 × ATR_20)
  - Early_session_opposite_move = first_hour_move_direction ≠ daily_trend_direction
  - Trend_day_candidate = Prior_2_days_range AND Early_session_opposite_move

# NOT_QUANT_YET

1. **Visual Pattern Recognition for Springs/Upthrusts**: The author describes "springs" and "upthrusts" conceptually in Wyckoff terms but these are visual chart patterns requiring human judgment or advanced ML.
2. **Market Memory of Swing Highs/Lows**: The text says market participants "remember" previous levels. This is a behavioral claim that can be proxied by price reaction at levels but not fully quantified.
3. **"Feel" for Momentum Exhaustion**: The author repeatedly references the feeling that momentum is insufficient. This is tacit knowledge developed through screen time.
4. **Dynamic Chart Timeframe Selection**: The author manually switches between 1m/2m/30s based on "feel" for volatility. No automatic rule is provided.
5. **Psychological State of "Flow"**: Trading in a state of flow is mentioned as critical. No quantifiable proxy.
