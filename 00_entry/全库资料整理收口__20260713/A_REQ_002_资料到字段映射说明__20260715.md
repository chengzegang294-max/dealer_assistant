### 主来源

- [郭永清对象入口](file:///d:/Stock/dealer_assistant/10_source_library_archive/topic_entry/object_entry/guo_yongqing_financial_statement_object_entry_v1.md)
- [第13章常见的相对估值法](file:///d:/Stock/dealer_assistant/10_source_library_archive/mirror_kimi_inbox/GROUP_10_A5_%E8%B4%A2%E6%8A%A5_%E4%BC%B0%E5%80%BC_%E7%BB%84%E5%90%88%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86/01_A5_cutpack_v1_final/%E8%B4%A2%E5%8A%A1%E6%8A%A5%E8%A1%A8%E5%88%86%E6%9E%90%E4%B8%8E%E8%82%A1%E7%A5%A8%E4%BC%B0%E5%80%BC_%E9%83%AD%E6%B0%B8%E6%B8%85/%E7%AC%AC13%E7%AB%A0%E5%B8%B8%E8%A7%81%E7%9A%84%E7%9B%B8%E5%AF%B9%E4%BC%B0%E5%80%BC%E6%B3%95.md)

### 当前可直接落的字段

- `value_pe_ttm`
  - 证据：`市盈率 = 每股价格 / 每股收益`
- `value_pb_lf`
  - 证据：`市净率 = 每股价格 / 每股净资产 = 股票市值 / 净资产`
- `value_ps_ttm`
  - 证据：章节明确把 `PS` 作为常见相对估值法之一
